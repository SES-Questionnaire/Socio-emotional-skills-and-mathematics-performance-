Socio-Emotional Skills Questionnaire for BSEd Mathematics Students

Research instrument for the study: Socio-Emotional Skills and Mathematics Performance of BSEd Mathematics Students

About the Questionnaire
This page documents the questionnaire used in the study Socio-Emotional Skills and Mathematics Performance of BSEd Mathematics Students. The questionnaire was developed and contextualized for the study using established theoretical frameworks and instruments. The questionnaire is organized according to the five competencies of the CASEL Social and Emotional Learning Framework.

The questionnaire's theoretical and contextual basis includes the Collaborative for Academic, Social, and Emotional Learning (CASEL) Social and Emotional Learning Framework and Philippine-focused work on socio-emotional skills by UNICEF Philippines and Dr. Ronnel B. King. The UNICEF Philippines publication reviews international and Philippine socio-emotional learning initiatives, including the CASEL framework, and discusses the development of socio-emotional skills assessment in the Philippine education context.

The questionnaire is organized according to the five competencies of the CASEL Social and Emotional Learning Framework:

Self-Awareness
Self-Management
Social Awareness
Relationship Skills
Responsible Decision-Making

Questionnaire Structure

1. Self-Awareness

The self-awareness dimension contains eight statements addressing students' recognition of their emotions, strengths, weaknesses, learning challenges, confidence, values, and academic goals in mathematics.

2. Self-Management

The self-management dimension contains eight statements addressing students' ability to regulate emotions, manage time, establish study goals, maintain motivation, sustain attention, manage academic stress, prepare for mathematics activities, and persist when facing difficult mathematical tasks.

3. Social Awareness

The social awareness dimension contains eight statements addressing students' awareness of classmates' experiences, respect for different mathematical approaches, understanding of diverse learning backgrounds, active listening, empathy, teamwork, classroom expectations, and responsiveness to instructor feedback.

4. Relationship Skills

The relationship skills dimension contains eight statements addressing collaboration, communication of mathematical ideas, seeking academic assistance, maintaining positive peer relationships, conflict resolution, constructive feedback, class participation, and leadership.

5. Responsible Decision-Making

The responsible decision-making dimension contains eight statements addressing students' consideration of consequences, study habits, checking mathematical solutions, responsibility for academic performance, logical decision-making, academic honesty, study planning, and prioritization of mathematics major subjects.

Response Scale

The questionnaire uses a five-point Likert-type response scale:

Scale	Response
5	Strongly Agree
4	Agree
3	Neutral
2	Disagree
1	Strongly Disagree

Theoretical Framework

The organization of the questionnaire follows the five core competencies of the Collaborative for Academic, Social, and Emotional Learning (CASEL) Social and Emotional Learning Framework.

The five competencies are:

Self-Awareness
Self-Management
Social Awareness
Relationship Skills
Responsible Decision-Making

Intended Context

The questionnaire was contextualized for BSEd Mathematics students and focuses on socio-emotional experiences related to mathematics learning, mathematics coursework, academic responsibilities, peer interaction, and preparation for the teaching profession.
