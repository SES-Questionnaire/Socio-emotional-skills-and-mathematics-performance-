Research Instrument Documentation

Socio-Emotional Skills Questionnaire

For BSEd Mathematics Students

Instrument Sources & Provenance

This page documents the questionnaire used in the study Socio-Emotional Skills and Mathematics Performance of BSEd Mathematics Students. The questionnaire was developed and contextualized for the study using established theoretical frameworks and instruments. The questionnaire is organized according to the five competencies of the CASEL Social and Emotional Learning Framework.

About the Questionnaire

This questionnaire measures socio-emotional skills in the context of BSEd Mathematics students. It is organized into five competencies: self-awareness, self-management, social awareness, relationship skills, and responsible decision-making.

Socio-Emotional Skills Items

Please indicate the response that best represents your answer to each statement.

1. Self-Awareness

  1. 1. I can accurately identify my feelings when solving math problems.
  2. 2. I am aware of my strengths and weaknesses in my major subjects.
  3. 3. I recognize how my emotions affect my focus during lectures.
  4. 4. I know which mathematical concepts I find most challenging.
  5. 5. I am confident in my ability to eventually learn difficult math topics.
  6. 6. I can tell when I am becoming frustrated with a derivation or proof.
  7. 7. I understand how my personal values influence my career choice as a math teacher.
  8. 8. I have a clear sense of my goals as a BSED Mathematics student.

2. Self-Management

  1. 1. I can stay calm when I encounter a math problem I cannot solve immediately.
  2. 2. I manage my time effectively to complete all my mathematics requirements.
  3. 3. I set specific study goals for my major subjects.
  4. 4. I can motivate myself to study even when the lesson is boring.
  5. 5. I stay focused in math class despite distractions from my surroundings.
  6. 6. I handle the stress of a heavy academic workload without burning out.
  7. 7. I prepare my materials and mindset before starting a math activity.
  8. 8. I control my impulsive urge to give up when a proof is too long.

3. Social Awareness

  1. 1. I can identify when my classmates are struggling with a math lesson.
  2. 2. I respect the different methods my peers use to solve the same problem.
  3. 3. I understand that my classmates come from diverse backgrounds and learning styles.
  4. 4. I listen actively when others explain their mathematical reasoning.
  5. 5. I am aware of the social norms and expectations in a professional classroom.
  6. 6. I feel empathy when a peer fails a major examination.
  7. 7. I recognize the importance of teamwork in accomplishing group projects.
  8. 8. I am sensitive to the feedback given by my mathematics instructors.

4. Relationship Skills

  1. 1. I work effectively with my peers during collaborative math activities.
  2. 2. I can explain complex math concepts to my classmates clearly.
  3. 3. I seek help from my professors when I do not understand a lesson.
  4. 4. I maintain positive relationships with my fellow BSED Math students.
  5. 5. I can resolve conflicts peacefully during group study sessions.
  6. 6. I provide constructive feedback to my peers on their work.
  7. 7. I am comfortable participating in class discussions and recitations.
  8. 8. I can lead a group effectively when assigned to a math project.

5. Responsible Decision-Making

  1. 1. I consider the consequences before deciding to skip a math lecture.
  2. 2. I choose study habits that I know will improve my GWA.
  3. 3. I evaluate the accuracy of a solution before submitting my paper.
  4. 4. I take responsibility for my poor performance in an exam instead of blaming others.
  5. 5. I make academic choices based on logical reasoning rather than peer pressure.
  6. 6. I follow the ethical standards of the university regarding academic honesty.
  7. 7. I identify potential problems in my study plan and fix them early.
  8. 8. I prioritize my major subjects because they are essential for my future board exam.

Framework Reference

The questionnaire is organized according to the five core competencies described in the Collaborative for Academic, Social, and Emotional Learning (CASEL) Social and Emotional Learning Framework.
